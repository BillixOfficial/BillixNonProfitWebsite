What we do

One layer over the systems you already use.

Billix wraps around the state's existing systems instead of replacing them. Grants still move directly from agency to utility, the state keeps its records, and caseworkers make every eligibility call. Billix handles the work in between.

The family front door

A single link or QR code opens a short mobile application. Screening questions route each household to the programs they qualify for, in the order the state requires. As families photograph their ID, bill, and income documents, the app checks each one and asks for a retake right away if anything is unreadable — so a missing page is fixed in seconds, not on a second trip in.

The caseworker workbench

Applications arrive ready to review. Documents are captured and legible, household income is calculated against the right program threshold, and anything that does not add up is flagged rather than buried. Caseworkers spend their time on decisions instead of assembling files and re-keying the same data twice.

The pledge tracker

When a grant is approved, Billix sends a standardized hold request to the utility through the channel it already uses, records the time it went out, and watches for confirmation. If a shutoff date is approaching without a response, it escalates on its own — so an approval never gets stranded in a shared inbox.

Who we serve

Billix meets each of them where they are — carrying the sequencing rules, eligibility charts, and utility-by-utility hold procedures that today live only in institutional memory.

Where we draw the line

Deliberately narrow, so we stay easy to adopt.

We don't hold funds

Grants flow from agency to utility exactly as they do today. Billix never sits in the money path, which keeps us clear of banking rules.

We're not the record

The state keeps its system of record. Billix formats verified data to match those screens and steps aside if a direct integration is ever granted.

We don't decide eligibility

The software computes and recommends; a caseworker makes every determination. Our job is making sure no one reviews an incomplete file.
